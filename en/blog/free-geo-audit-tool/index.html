What is GEO visibility and why measure it?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) prepares your website so artificial intelligence can find and recommend you. More and more people ask ChatGPT or Gemini directly instead of Google, and if those models don't understand your site, you simply don't show up.

Measuring your AI presence is the logical first step: you need to know how a generative engine sees you today before you can improve it. Just as SEO exists for Google, GEO now exists for AI.

Measure your AI visibility for free

We built a free online tool to audit it: geo.paginaswebtotal.com. It analyzes your site the way an AI engine would and gives you a 0-100 score with concrete recommendations.

It checks the signals that matter to the models: your robots.txt for bots like GPTBot and PerplexityBot, your structured data (Schema.org), your llms.txt file, AI discovery endpoints and content freshness. The analysis is deterministic and uses no tokens, which is why it's 100% free and requires no signup.

How to measure your GEO visibility in 3 steps

1. Open the free tool at geo.paginaswebtotal.com.

2. Paste your website address and click analyze.

3. In seconds you get your score and the list of improvements so AI starts citing you.
