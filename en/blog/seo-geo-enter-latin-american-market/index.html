Why Latin America is an opportunity now

Latin America has 650M+ people and one of the fastest-growing rates of mobile internet and e-commerce adoption worldwide, on two dominant languages — Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. For a foreign company with a strong product, the bottleneck is rarely demand: it is local visibility.

The most expensive mistake: translating instead of localizing

Translating your site into Spanish is not entering the market. Each country searches differently and Brazilian Portuguese is practically a separate commercial language. Real localization adapts keywords, search intent, currency and the local signals Google and AI use.

SEO + GEO: trust in a new market

When you enter a country where nobody knows your brand, trust is everything. SEO makes you appear in the region's Google results; GEO makes ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity cite you directly — an instant recommendation.

How to position your company in 4 steps

3. Authority and citation: local backlinks and AI-extractable content.
